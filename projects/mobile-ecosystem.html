The context

Programming Hub has maintained a portfolio of mobile applications serving learners. A shared codebase covering approximately 40 Android app flavors brings benefits of reuse while creating coordination challenges for configuration, feature consistency and releases.

My role at RightSol encompasses technical delivery across the mobile product ecosystem as part of the Head of Tech Team remit.

What I owned and contributed

  • Maintaining and evolving product features and configurations across a multi-flavor Android portfolio.
  • Coordinating technical decisions, app publishing requirements and production issue resolution.
  • Working with Android, Flutter, Firebase, analytics and subscription-related product workflows.
  • Collaborating with cross-functional stakeholders on product delivery and quality priorities.

Engineering conversations

This experience provides concrete discussion topics for architecture and technical interviews:

  • How to balance shared code with application-level configuration.
  • What changes deserve centralized implementations versus flavor-specific behavior.
  • How to reason about Gradle configuration, build performance and safe release planning.
  • How crash data, app reviews and product requirements inform priorities.
This is a public-facing summary of engineering responsibilities, not a disclosure of RightSol’s private source code, infrastructure or architecture diagrams.

Historical product milestones

The previous resume records that Programming Hub had reported 5M+ users, product reliability had reached 99.7% crash-free users, and ratings had improved from 4.0 to above 4.7. These are historical figures and are not represented as current performance.
